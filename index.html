Questions

Good to know.

Where do you play?

I'm based in Virginia Beach and play weddings across Hampton Roads, including Norfolk, Chesapeake, Hampton, Newport News, Portsmouth and Suffolk. Celebrations beyond Hampton Roads are welcome on request.

Can you play our song if you don't already know it?

Yes. Tell me the songs that matter to you, whether that's the processional, the recessional or your first dance, and I'll learn and arrange them for your day.

Do you need a piano at the venue?

No. I can play the venue's piano, or bring and set up my own professional instrument.

Is a rehearsal included?

Yes. Every collection includes a dress rehearsal at no extra charge, so the music is timed to your ceremony before the day.

Can we have a full band instead of solo piano?

Yes. The live band adds drums and live vocals to the keys, from the ceremony to the last dance. Every band wedding is quoted around your day.

Our wedding is less than two weeks away. Can you still play?

Often, yes. Short-notice dates are welcome when my schedule allows, and a rush fee applies to cover learning your music quickly.

Do you play events other than weddings?

Yes. I also play proposals, private parties and corporate events.
