About

Music that moves with your moment

I'm Joshua Seth-Buachie, a professional keyboardist based in Virginia Beach specializing in live wedding music. Whether it's the processional that brings your guests to tears or the cocktail hour soundtrack that sets the mood for the reception, I bring warmth, precision, and a personal touch to every performance.

With Buachie Entertainment, you get a musician who listens — to your story, your song choices, and the feeling you want your guests to carry home.

Dress rehearsals are always included at no additional charge. Your confidence on the big day is part of the service.
